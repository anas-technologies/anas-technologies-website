03 / PROJECT WORKFLOW

Development Process

Understand how a typical project moves from an idea toward launch.

What is your development process?

Our approach generally follows four stages: discovery, design, development, and launch. Testing, feedback, and revisions are incorporated as agreed for each project.

How long does it take to complete a project?

Timelines depend on the project's complexity, features, integrations, content readiness, and review requirements. We discuss an estimated schedule after reviewing the project scope.

Can I provide feedback during development?

Yes. Client feedback is an important part of our collaborative process. Specific review points and revision arrangements are established during project planning.
