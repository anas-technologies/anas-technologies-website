Our Privacy Commitment

ANAS Technologies aims to be transparent about how personal information is handled when visitors interact with our website and services.

01 / OVERVIEW

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ANAS Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") handles personal information in connection with our website, inquiries, and digital services.

It is intended to help visitors understand the types of information we may collect, the purposes for which it may be used, and the choices available regarding personal information.

02 / DATA COLLECTION

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, the following categories of information may be collected:

  • Contact information: Your name, email address, phone number, or other details submitted through an inquiry.
  • Project information: Requirements, service preferences, budgets, and details you choose to share when requesting a quotation.
  • Technical information: Where applicable, browser information, device type, and website usage data.
  • Communication information: Messages exchanged with our team through available contact channels.
03 / DATA USAGE

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our website may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to questions and project inquiries.
  • Preparing quotations and service proposals.
  • Communicating about requested services.
  • Operating and maintaining website functionality.
  • Improving website content and user experience.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Marketing communications, where offered, should be sent in accordance with applicable requirements and the preferences expressed by recipients.

04 / INFORMATION SHARING

Sharing Your Information

Personal information may need to be processed by third-party service providers supporting website hosting, communications, or business operations, where those services are used.

Information may also need to be disclosed where legally required. The specific providers, purposes, and safeguards applicable to your information depend on the services actually used by ANAS Technologies.

05 / COOKIES

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small data files that websites may place on a visitor's device to support functionality, remember preferences, or analyze website usage.

Any cookies used by this website, including those set by embedded or third-party services, should be described in the applicable Cookies Policy.

06 / SECURITY

Data Security

ANAS Technologies recognizes the importance of protecting personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

No website or electronic transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Visitors should avoid submitting passwords, payment credentials, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary inquiry forms.

07 / RETENTION

How Long We Keep Information

Retention periods should reflect the purpose for which information was collected, applicable legal requirements, and legitimate business needs.
