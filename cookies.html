Understanding Your Choices

This page explains common cookie technologies and the choices available to website visitors. Actual cookie usage depends on the services enabled on the website.

01 / OVERVIEW

Introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how cookies and similar tracking technologies may be used on the ANAS Technologies website.

It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which provides further information about personal information and privacy requests.

02 / THE BASICS

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a visitor's device through their web browser.

They can help websites maintain sessions, remember preferences, support certain features, or understand how visitors interact with web pages.

Other technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, may serve related purposes but are not technically cookies.

03 / CATEGORIES

Types of Cookies

The following categories describe common cookie purposes. Their inclusion here does not mean every category is currently used by our website.

01

Essential Cookies

Support functions necessary for website operation, security, or requested functionality.

02

Preference Cookies

May remember visitor choices such as language or display preferences.

03

Analytics Cookies

May help website operators understand traffic patterns and page interactions.

04

Marketing Cookies

May be used by advertising technologies to measure campaigns or personalize advertisements.

04 / PURPOSES

How Cookies May Be Used

Depending on the features and external services installed, cookies or similar technologies may support:

  • Basic website functionality and security.
  • Remembering selected preferences.
  • Understanding technical website performance.
  • Measuring engagement with website content.
  • Supporting third-party functionality.

The specific cookies present on the published website should be verified through a technical cookie audit.

05 / EXTERNAL SERVICES

Third-Party Services

Our website includes links to external services, including social media and WhatsApp. Opening these external websites may subject visitors to the providers' own technologies and privacy policies.

Third-party scripts, embedded content, analytics services, or advertising tools may also use cookies if such features are added to the website.

Visitors should review the applicable third-party policies for further details.

06 / USER CONTROL

How to Manage Cookies

Most web browsers provide settings for viewing, blocking, or deleting cookies.

  • Review your browser's privacy and security settings.
  • Delete stored cookies where desired.
  • Block third-party cookies if supported.
  • Adjust browser permissions and privacy controls.

Blocking certain cookies may affect the operation of features that depend on them.

07 / CONSENT

Cookie Consent

Where applicable law requires consent before optional cookies or similar tracking technologies are used, an appropriate consent mechanism should be implemented before those technologies operate.

A Cookies Policy page alone does not provide a working consent mechanism or automatically disable third-party tracking technologies.

08 / DURATION

How Long Cookies Remain

Cookie duration varies by type and provider.

  • Session cookies: Usually expire when the browser session ends.
  • Persistent cookies: May remain until their specified expiration date or until deleted through browser settings.

Specific cookie names, providers, and expiration periods should be confirmed against the live website.

09 / REVISIONS

Updates to This Policy

This Cookies Policy may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, third-party technologies, or legal requirements.

The effective date should be updated when a revised policy is published.
