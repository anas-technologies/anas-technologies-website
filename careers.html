UI/UX Designer

Design • Remote / Islamabad • Full-time

About the Role

We're looking for a creative UI/UX Designer who is passionate about crafting beautiful, user-centered experiences. You'll work closely with our team to design intuitive interfaces that solve real user problems.

Responsibilities

  • Design user-centered interfaces for web and mobile applications
  • Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups
  • Conduct user research and usability testing
  • Collaborate with developers and product managers
  • Ensure consistency in design systems and UI libraries

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a UI/UX Designer or similar role
  • Proficiency in Figma, Adobe XD, or similar tools
  • Strong understanding of design principles and user experience
  • Portfolio showcasing your design projects
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills

Job Details

Job Type Full-time
Location Remote / Islamabad
Experience 1 - 3 Years
Salary PKR 80,000 - 120,000
